Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

MSR Innovations LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through www.msrinnovations.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your business name (optional)
  • The content of your message

If you contact us directly by email or phone, we receive the information included in that communication.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and schedule consultations
  • Provide and discuss our services with you
  • Keep records of our communications

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted third parties to run the Site and handle communications:

  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the Site.
  • Web3Forms processes contact form submissions and forwards them to our email.
  • Zoho provides our email service.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts used on the Site.

These providers only process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy policies.

Cookies

The Site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Data retention

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and maintain business records, and then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@msrinnovations.com.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children's privacy

The Site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
